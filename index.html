5 Our story

A small café that brings people together

  1. Roll Mi began with a simple idea: a small Hanoi café where people could eat, relax and spend time together.

  2. We began as a café with board games and grew into Roll Mi, bringing Vietnamese bánh mì, coffee and games together.

  3. We treasure returning guests, conversations around a game and travellers who find us along the way.

  4. Come with friends, your partner or family, and make Roll Mi part of your Hanoi memories.

Before you visit

Do I have to pay to play board games?

There’s no separate game fee. Each guest needs to order at least one food or drink item.

Is there a time limit for playing?

No. We don’t set a time limit for board game play during opening hours.

Can I play if I’ve never tried board games before?

Of course. Ask our team to recommend a game for your group and walk you through the basic rules.

Should a large group book ahead?

Message Roll Mi on Facebook before visiting with a large group so we can prepare seating.
